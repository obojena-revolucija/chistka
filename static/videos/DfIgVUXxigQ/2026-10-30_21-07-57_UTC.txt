Patriotizam 🤍 For two years, the students of Serbia turned their grief and outrage into a relentless battle for justice, building a movement so powerful that it didn’t just topple a regime - it became a global blueprint and an inspiration for youth resistance everywhere.

Now, the elections are over, and the real work begins. 
Instead of just celebrating, our community took to the streets and underground passages across Serbia. Armed with sponges and water, regular people spent their morning washing away months of political graffiti and hate speech that was encouraged for 15 years by the former regime.
Winning an election is just the first step. Healing our society and taking care of the streets we share that is where true change lives. This isn’t just about politics, it’s about a generation deciding that hate no longer belongs on our walls.
This is what civic love looks like.
