Belgrade residents washed away politically charged graffiti that had stained city walls during months of protests, as Serbia's student-led movement prepare to govern after its election victory. ⁣
⁣
A student-backed list of politicians defeated the right-wing nationalist party of Aleksandar Vučić in a national election on 25 October, ending its 14-year rule. ⁣
⁣
The victory came after two years of protests sparked by the 2024 collapse of a railway station canopy in Novi Sad that claimed 16 lives. Students also removed portraits of Vučić from some government buildings. ⁣
