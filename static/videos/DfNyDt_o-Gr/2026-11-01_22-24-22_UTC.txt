After 14 years of SNS rule, and following the student list’s victory, it finally feels like a new chapter is beginning — one that leaves room for people who want to contribute to their country and society. 🇷🇸🇷🇸🇷🇸

Across Serbia, people are channelling their positive energy into collective volunteer actions, coming together to improve their local communities and take matters into their own hands.

One such initiative was yesterday’s repainting of the underpass in Dorćol, where people gathered to cover up the ugly, hate-filled messages that had been painted on its walls. The action was organised by @zavrnirukave and it is just one of many past initiatives, with many more to come. For updates on upcoming actions, give them a follow!

A little paint, a lot of goodwill, some music and, of course, rakija. Sometimes, rebuilding a society starts with simply showing up and doing something together. ❤️🇷🇸
